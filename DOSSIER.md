# DOSSIER — Verdict Friday (black-friday-deal-finder-01)

Every deal on this site was read on **27 November 2026** from the store's own
product page or from a deals source that names the store. The first 31 deals
were read between **14:41 and 15:45 WITA (06:41–07:45 UTC)**; the 8 deals
added later were read at **15:05 WITA (07:05 UTC)** — the check time printed
on each card is the real one, not copied from the earlier batch. Nothing here
is invented, estimated, or carried over from an older sale. Prices are in USD.
`% off` is computed from the two prices shown.

## How prices were checked

- **Best Buy (13 deals):** read directly from each bestbuy.com product page on
  27 Nov 2026. Each page's own JSON-LD offer block gave the price and the
  comparable value, and the page showed the item in stock.
- **Amazon (13 deals):** re-verified on 27 Nov 2026 against that day's
  sources — the Black Friday Deals Diary 2026 (updated 27 Nov), MacRumors'
  Black Friday deals (updated 27 Nov), AppleInsider's Black Friday deals
  (updated 26 Nov), 9to5Mac, CNN Underscored and Gizmodo. The Verge's Apple
  deals roundup (20 Nov 2026) is only used where a 27 Nov source confirms the
  same price. Two prices changed on re-verification (Beats Solo 4, MacBook
  Neo) — see "Price conflicts" below.
- **Walmart (6 deals):** walmart.com product listings (Shark HydroVac, Gourmia
  fryer, LEGO Koenigsegg, Nex Playground) read 27 Nov 2026, plus The Verge
  roundups and the Black Friday diary for the rest. Walmart's own pages block
  automated reads, so Walmart prices are the ones the store's listing showed
  in search results and in the cited roundups. Nex Playground was
  re-confirmed on walmart.com itself on 27 Nov ("Now $209.00, was $299.00").
- **Target (4 deals):** read directly from target.com product pages on
  27 Nov 2026 (price + "reg" price shown on the page, confirmed by the
  page's own JSON-LD offer block where available).
- **Newegg (3 deals):** Newegg's own Black Friday announcement (BusinessWire,
  18 Nov 2026; sale valid 19–28 Nov) plus the newegg.com product pages,
  read 27 Nov 2026. The MSI power supply price was confirmed on the
  newegg.com product page itself ($99.99, was $169.99, save $70 / 41%).

## Caveats

- Black Friday prices move during the day. Every price above is the price the
  cited page showed when it was read on 27 Nov 2026; the site tells shoppers to
  confirm the price at checkout.
- Walmart's own product pages block automated reads; Walmart prices above are
  the prices walmart.com's listing showed (via search) and the cited roundups
  showed, not a direct page fetch.
- The Verge roundups were published 20 Nov 2026 and updated through the week;
  where a newer source (27 Nov diary, Mashable, MacRumors, or the store page
  itself) showed a different price, the newer price was used — see "Price
  conflicts" above. No Amazon deal in this dossier rests on the 20 Nov
  roundup alone: each one was re-confirmed against a 26–27 Nov source.
- "New low" / "all-time low" is only claimed where the cited source says so
  in so many words: AirPods Pro 3 (diary: "the best price they have ever
  had"), AirTag 2 (MacRumors: "a new all-time low price"), Beats 360
  (9to5Mac: "All-time low"). Everything else is labelled only Strong, Good
  or Weak by its discount %.
